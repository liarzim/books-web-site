The story is narrated by Nick Carraway, a young man from Minnesota who moves to New York in the summer of 1922. Nick rents a house next to the mansion of Jay Gatsby, a mysterious millionaire who throws lavish parties but does not participate in them.

Gatsby's obsessive love for Daisy Buchanan drives the novel toward its tragic conclusion, offering a searing critique of the American Dream along the way.
