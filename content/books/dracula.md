When Jonathan Harker travels to Transylvania to finalize a property transaction with the mysterious Count Dracula, he quickly realizes his host is not what he seems.

Told entirely through diary entries, letters, and telegrams, *Dracula* follows a small band of friends as they race to stop the Count's plan to spread his curse to England.
